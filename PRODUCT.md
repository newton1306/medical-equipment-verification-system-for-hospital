# Product

## Register
product

## Users
CSSD staff in hospitals. The context is a fast-paced, sterile environment where they need quick, unambiguous feedback.

## Product Purpose
Instantly verify surgical instrument trays before sterilization (PASS/FAIL/UNCERTAIN) using AI.

## Brand Personality
Clinical precision, clarity, speed, trust.

## Anti-references
Should NOT look like a generic SaaS dashboard or a playful consumer app. No glassmorphism, no bouncy animations.

## Design Principles
1. Extreme clarity (PASS/FAIL must be instantly readable from a distance)
2. High contrast for clinical environments
3. Unambiguous interaction (buttons must look like actions)

## Accessibility & Inclusion
High contrast for quick reading; large tap targets for gloved hands or mobile usage.
